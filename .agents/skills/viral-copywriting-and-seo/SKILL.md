# Viral Copywriting & SEO Skill

This skill guides Antigravity agents in crafting high-conversion metadata, click-worthy titles, dynamic hashtags, and compliant descriptions tailored for the YouTube Shorts algorithm.

---

## 1. Curiosity-Gap Hook Architecture

Every title must create an immediate psychological gap in the viewer's mind that can only be satisfied by watching the Short until the end.

### Golden Rules:
1. **Length**: 40 to 60 characters (fits cleanly on mobile feeds without ellipsis cut-off).
2. **Casing**: ALL CAPS for high urgency and feed contrast.
3. **Emotional Trigger Words**: `ACCIDENTALLY`, `TERRIFYING`, `IMPOSSIBLE`, `NEVER SEEN`, `INSTANT KARMA`, `BRO DID NOT`.
4. **Emoji Hygiene**: Never put emojis directly inside FFmpeg text render overlays to avoid square box glitches (`□`), but DO include 1-2 relevant emojis in the YouTube title and description metadata!

### Formula Templates:
- **Streamer / Chaos**: `[CREATOR] ACCIDENTALLY BROKE EVERYTHING! 💀`
- **Instant Karma**: `HE THOUGHT HE WAS SAFE... INSTANT KARMA NEVER MISSES 😭`
- **Physics / Destruction**: `THIS CAR CRASH PHYSICS ENGINE IS ACTUALLY INSANE! 🚗💥`
- **Cinema Climax**: `THE MOMENT EVERYONE HELD THEIR BREATH IN THE THEATER... 🎬🔥`
- **Sports Miracle**: `HE HIT A HALF-COURT SHOT WITH 0.1 SECONDS LEFT! 🏀🚨`

## 2. Dynamic Hashtag Rotation Protocol

> **[CRITICAL ANTI-SPAM RULE] NEVER output identical hashtag sets across consecutive uploads.**
> Repeating identical hashtag strings triggers YouTube's internal spam filters and suppresses recommendation reach.

### 5-Tier Hashtag Assembly Formula:
Every uploaded Short must receive 5-6 hashtags assembled using this dynamic matrix:

1. **Category Tag**: `#Gaming`, `#Cinema`, `#UFC`, `#NBA`, `#Satisfying`
2. **Specific Entity Tag**: `#GTA6`, `#IShowSpeed`, `#EldenRing`, `#JohnWick`, `#LethalCompany`
3. **Scene Action Tag**: `#BuzzerBeater`, `#PoliceEscape`, `#GamingFail`, `#InstantKarma`, `#EpicDrop`
4. **Viral Format Tag**: `#Shorts`, `#ViralShorts`, `#TrendingShorts`
5. **Dopamine Tag**: `#MindBlown`, `#WaitTillTheEnd`, `#PlotTwist`

## 3. Fair Use & Copyright Compliance Architecture

Every video uploaded by Money Glitch Autonomous Studio strictly observes YouTube Fair Use policies:
- **Transformative Value**: Added commentary, kinetic typography, OLED grading, and interstitial comedic editing.
- **Credit Attribution**: Creator channel name and original video source are always credited in the first lines of the description.
- **Standard Legal Disclaimer**:
  ```text
  Credit to original creator: [CREATOR_NAME]
  Original Video: [SOURCE_TITLE]

  This video is transformed under YouTube Fair Use (Section 107 of the US Copyright Act) for commentary, humor, and educational entertainment.
  ```
